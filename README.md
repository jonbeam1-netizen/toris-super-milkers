# Tori's Super Milkers

A cute 3D milk-farming RPG that runs in your browser.

**Play it:** https://jonbeam1-netizen.github.io/toris-super-milkers/

- Start with one cow and one black sheep.
- Explore a dense forest full of trails, clearings and roaming kittens to find 11 more kinds of cow (chocolate, strawberry, banana, blueberry, orange, almond, mochi, mint chip, cookies & cream, moonlight, golden) and whiter sheep. Befriend them and lead them home.
- The whiter the fleece, the better the sheep. Brush them and shear Snow and Radiant wool.
- Feed wool to the Great Spindle in the Sheep-a-verse, then weave the yarn into the force field that protects the castle city from nightly kitten raids.
- Pick up a cow and squirt a stream of milk from her udder. Every milk does something different.
- Date the farmers of Buttercup Hollow by solving their milk problems.
- Bring all 12 cows home to fuse them into the Super Milker Cow, defeat King Clawdius in Catnap Cavern, free the Seraph Sheep, then survive endless raids.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Walk (Shift to run) |
| E | Main action |
| Space | Jump (over fences too) |
| 1 2 3 | Other actions |
| C | Pick up / put down a cow |
| Hold F or mouse | Squirt milk |
| B / I / Q / H / M | Herd Book / Bag / Quests / Help / Sound |

On phones, drag on the left side to walk and use the buttons on the right.

Progress saves in your browser.
