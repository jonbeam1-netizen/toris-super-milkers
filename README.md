# Tori's Super Milkers

A cute 3D milk-farming RPG that runs in your browser.

**Play it:** https://jonbeam1-netizen.github.io/toris-super-milkers/

- Milk 12 kinds of cows: fresh, chocolate, strawberry, banana, blueberry, orange, mochi, almond, mint chip, cookies & cream, moonlight and golden.
- The whiter the fleece, the better the sheep. Brush them, feed clover, and shear Snow and Radiant wool.
- Feed wool to the Great Spindle in the Sheep-a-verse, then weave the yarn into the force field that protects the castle city from evil kittens.
- Pick up a cow and squirt a stream of milk from her udder. Every milk does something different.
- Date the farmers of Buttercup Hollow by solving their milk problems.
- Mix every milk into Super Milker Milk to defeat King Clawdius in Catnap Cavern.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| WASD / arrows | Walk (Shift to run) |
| E / Space | Main action |
| 1 2 3 | Other actions |
| C | Pick up / put down a cow |
| Hold F or mouse | Squirt milk |
| I / Q / H / M | Bag / Quests / Help / Sound |

On phones, drag on the left side to walk and use the buttons on the right.

Progress saves in your browser.
